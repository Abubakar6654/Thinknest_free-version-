✨ Core Features

Everything a Student Needs to Thrive

From AI tutoring to teacher content and brain training — ThinkNest brings the whole school into one place.

🤖
AI Tutor
A chat-based tutor that answers questions anytime, explains in simple language, and guides students step by step.
🎯
Diagnostic Assessment
A short entry test identifies each student's strengths and gaps, then builds their personalized learning path automatically.
📚
Adaptive Learning Path
Difficulty adjusts based on performance. Weak areas get more attention. Every student moves at their own pace.
🧑‍🏫
Teacher Content System
Teachers create and upload lessons, quizzes, and notes. Students access human-crafted content alongside AI materials.
🧠
Daily Brain Training
Short exercises for memory, logic, problem-solving, and reading comprehension — every single day.
📊
Progress Dashboard
Students and parents see lesson completions, quiz scores, streaks, and performance trends in real time.
🏆
Gamification
Points, badges, levels, and streak rewards keep students motivated and coming back every day.
👨‍👩‍👧
Parent Portal
Parents create and manage profiles, monitor progress, and stay informed — all without sitting beside their child.
